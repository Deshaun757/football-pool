Support & privacy requests

Last updated September 6, 2026 · Version 2026-09-06

Huddle Pick’em is operated by Redcrows Solutions, Norfolk, Virginia, United States. Contact support@huddlepickem.com for support, account access, privacy, or security concerns.

Getting help

For group approvals and settings, start with your commissioner. For app problems, include your account email, group name, week and entry number if relevant, what happened, and what you expected. Never send passwords, session cookies, or password-reset links. If you cannot sign in, try Forgot password or email support from your account address.

Privacy and account deletion

You may request access to your information, correction, account deletion, or review of another privacy issue. Signed-in requests are recorded below for the app administrator; you can also email support. Deletion requires review and is not immediate. The operator may verify identity, consider commissioner responsibilities, and explain any records retained for other players’ results, disputes, or applicable obligations. Do not include sensitive documents unless support explains a necessary verification step.

Sign in, then return here to change email preferences or submit and track a request. You do not need to join a group first.

Email preferences

These choices apply across your groups. Welcome, password-reset, invitation, and pick-decision messages are separate service messages. Messages already handed to your email provider cannot be recalled.

Submit a request

Submitting a deletion request does not immediately remove access or delete records. Up to five requests per account per day.

Your requests

Administrator request inbox

Review the request and contact the requester through the support mailbox as needed. Perform and document the appropriate action before marking resolved. This status control does not delete an account, export data, or send a reply.

Security and availability

Report suspected account access or exposed information privately by email with a concise description. Avoid sending another player’s personal information unnecessarily. Support is reviewed by the operator; there is no staffed emergency or guaranteed instant-response channel. For questions about deadlines, do not wait for an email before taking an available in-app action.
